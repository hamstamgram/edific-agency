FAQ

Pricing questions, answered

What's included in the monthly price?
Everything: system build, deployment, hosting, monitoring, monthly optimization, and support. You pay one flat fee — no surprise costs for "maintenance" or "updates."
What if the AI doesn't deliver the expected results?
Your first month is a 30-day pilot. We agree on specific KPIs upfront. If we don't hit them, you don't pay the setup fee. We only keep clients who see real ROI.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. No long-term contracts. Month-to-month pricing. Cancel with 30 days notice. We'll even help you transition any systems you want to keep running.
How is this different from hiring a VA or employee?
A VA costs €2,500–4,000/month, works 8 hours/day, needs training, and makes mistakes. Our systems cost less, work 24/7, never forget, and improve over time. Most clients replace 1–2 ops roles within 90 days.
Do you offer discounts for annual commitments?
Yes. Annual plans get 2 months free (effectively ~17% discount). Ask during your strategy call.
What's the Enterprise "Custom" pricing range?
Enterprise typically starts around €5,000–€8,000/month depending on complexity, number of workflows, integrations required, and SLA level. We'll give you a firm quote during the discovery call — no surprises.
