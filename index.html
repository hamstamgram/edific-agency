FAQ

Common questions.

Does Edific replace my front desk receptionist?
No. Edific handles the intake layer — answering calls, messages, qualifying patients, booking appointments. Your front desk still handles check-ins, patient relations, billing questions, and everything that benefits from a human touch. It gives them hours back in their day.
What happens if Edific doesn't understand a patient?
Edific detects uncertainty or repeated clarification requests and gracefully escalates to your team. It logs the full conversation so your staff picks up with zero context loss. The system learns from every escalation.
How fast is the response time?
Sub-2 seconds on every channel. Phone calls are answered before the second ring. WhatsApp messages respond instantly. Patients never wait.
What languages does Edific support?
German, English, Turkish, Arabic, and Polish out of the box. Additional languages can be added on request. Edific handles code-switching naturally — a patient can start in German and switch to English mid-conversation.
Which PMS and calendar systems do you integrate with?
Dentally, Zahn+, coag, and any Standards-kompatibel system. For other PMS platforms, we provide a universal API integration layer. If your system has an API, we connect to it.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. No lock-in contracts. Month-to-month. If Edific isn't delivering measurable results within 30 days, we refund you in full. No questions asked.
